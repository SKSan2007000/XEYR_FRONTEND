# XEYR Technologies frontend prototype

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step is required.

## Included
- Animated XEYR welcome screen and responsive navigation
- Moving announcement banner and focus-area ticker
- Photo-led carousel for talks, workshops, hackathons and technology teams
- SolveX and XEYR SkillBridge service cards
- Workshop/event registration-interest modal
- College service enquiry modal with service and payment-preference selection
- SkillBridge professional application modal with skills, profile and optional resume selection
- Success confirmation popups and responsive accessible layouts

## Important before production
This is a frontend prototype, not a live backend. Form entries are saved in the visitor's browser `localStorage` only. They do not reach XEYR by email or a central database. The resume selection is not uploaded. Payment preference is collected for follow-up only: no payment is processed. Before launch, connect a backend/database, email notifications, secure resume storage, privacy/consent handling and a payment gateway if you intend to collect payments. Replace sample event listings with confirmed names, dates, fees and venues.

## Branding
The uploaded XEYR brand assets are included under `assets/`. SolveX and XEYR SkillBridge logos are lightweight CSS-based draft marks.
